3461 South New York Avenue  ·  Kitchen Window Above Sink

Project Overview

This assessment covers the kitchen window above the sink at the Mooney residence — a single opening, currently a double-hung unit, with a great view over the backyard. We're providing two quote paths so you can compare cost and disruption before deciding: a pocket fit insert replacement, and a full-frame replacement, both as a new awning-style window.

An awning window hinges at the top and opens outward from the bottom, which is a great fit for a sink location — it lets you keep the window cracked open for ventilation even in light rain, without anything blocking the counter or sink area below it.

Two paths, one decision point. Pocket fit reuses your existing frame — faster install, less disruption, exterior finished in white aluminum trim. Full frame replaces everything down to the rough opening — more work, but addresses any hidden issues in the old frame and gets a completely fresh start with matching interior trim.
