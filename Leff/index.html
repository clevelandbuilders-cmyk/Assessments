Garage Door Trim
Exterior · 6228 60th Avenue
Garage Door Trim Replacement

The existing wood trim around both overhead garage door openings will be removed and replaced with AZEK PVC trim and PVC brickmold, painted Eldridge Gray. AZEK won't rot, split, or soak up water, so it holds paint and stands up at ground level where wood trim takes the most abuse. Both openings are the same size and get the same treatment on three sides — two legs and the head, including the clipped corners at the top.

Remove existing wood trim on three sides of both garage openings
Install new AZEK 1x6 trim on the legs and head
Install new AZEK PVC brickmold against the brick on the legs and head
Cut and fit the clipped (angled) corners at the top of each opening
Painted Eldridge Gray
Seal and caulk joints to the brick and the door frame
Haul away old trim and clean up

Exterior · Both Garages
Garage Door Weatherstrip Replacement

The weatherstrip that seals against the face of the garage door will be replaced on both openings along with the trim. New weatherstrip keeps drafts, water, and pests out along the sides and top of the door.

Remove existing weatherstrip on both legs and the head of each opening
Install new garage door weatherstrip, set tight to the closed door

Kitchen Pantry Closet
Interior · Kitchen
Pantry Closet Replacement & Reinforcement

The kitchen pantry closet will be rebuilt and reinforced with prefinished cabinet-grade plywood. The UV-coated finish is applied at the factory, so the shelving is ready to use as soon as it's in. Everything is cut to fit on site.

Remove existing pantry shelving
Replace and reinforce with 5/8" UV-coated prefinished cabinet plywood
Cut and fit on site
Clean up and haul away old material
