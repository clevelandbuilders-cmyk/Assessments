What This Job Really Is

Jason, I walked this house on Saturday, October 10. Chris Hanish called me about replacement windows, but the house is taking water in several places and the real job is the whole exterior plus interior repairs. I'm sending it your way as a referral.

What I found and talked through with him
Every window in the house needs replacing. Two are really bad, and second-floor windows are leaking into the first-floor ceilings.
The roof was redone about 10 years ago. I don't think it was flashed properly where the siding meets the roof.
The cedar siding is at the point of tear-off and replace.
The back patio door has a soft spot from water getting in.
Inside, there is ceiling drywall to cut out and two bathrooms with their own leak damage, one with rotting subfloor.
There has not been much maintenance done on this house and it is starting to show. He has also talked about cutting his losses and selling it as-is.
