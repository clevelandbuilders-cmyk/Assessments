Overview
Basement · Bathroom · Layout v2.9
Project at a Glance

The bathroom is already framed in the basement: a 10'-6" square room with a 91¼" ceiling. The plan turns it into a dark, moody spa bath. A steam shower for two and a cedar sauna sit side by side behind one continuous wall of glass along the built-in wall. A 48" floating walnut vanity sits on the stair wall, and a separate toilet room tucks under the stairs.

Steam Shower
Wet Zone · Crawlspace End
Steam Shower for Two

The steam shower takes the first 66" of the built-in wall, at the foam-board end. Inside it's about 64" x 52", comfortable for two people standing. One person takes the rain head while the other uses the handshower, or you share. The steam generator lives in the built-ins on the living-room side, right behind the shower.

Floor-to-ceiling large-format charcoal stone-look porcelain, matte with subtle veining
One 40" x 14" recessed niche on the back wall, sill at ~42", warm 2700K wet-rated LED under the top edge
One 14" round brushed brass rain head on a ceiling arm, toward the bench end
Brushed brass slide-bar handshower and 2-outlet control near the partition end
Steam head about 12" off the floor, away from the bench
16"-deep floating bench across the 52" end wall, seats two
Linear drain along the back wall; floor slopes in one plane at ¼" per foot
Ceiling at 84", sloped about 2" toward the back wall so condensation runs down the wall
Steam-rated waterproof membrane (0.5 perm or less) on walls, ceiling and niche
Glass to the ceiling, 24" door with a small operable transom to air it out

Fixtures & Shopping List

Every visible metal piece is Vibrant Brushed Moderne Brass (finish code -2MB): rain head, handshower, shower control, steam head, faucets and glass hardware. Sinks and the toilet stay Black Black china. The warehouse boxes are plain white, so match both the model number and the finish code on each label.

How to read a Kohler box

2MBFinish
The last part is always the finish or color. That's the part to check against this list. The model number in the middle tells you what the product is. A letter between them is a version: G means lower water flow, K1 means a kit.
A T in front of the model, like K-T14413-4-2MB, means trim only: just the part you see, with the in-wall valve sold separately. A number right after the model, like the -4 or -9, is part of the model (a handle or control style), not the color.

Mechanicals
Behind the walls
Plumbing, Electrical & Ventilation
Plumbing

The sewer leaves about 5 ft up, so everything drains by gravity under the slab to the existing ejector crock on the laundry side of the stairs.

One new 3" line under the slab from the shower drain, along the foam-board wall, past the toilet room, into the crock (~12–15 ft, ~3–4" of fall at ¼" per foot)
Replace the existing pump (not sewage-rated) with a ½ HP sewage ejector that passes 2" solids
Confirm 2" discharge; keep a check valve plus a full-port shutoff, the gasketed lid and the vent; add a high-water alarm
Confirm the basin is big enough (18" x 30" is typical); swap it while the floor is open if not
Steam generator drain and relief line tie into a drain through an air gap
Linear drain on the back wall; optional small sauna floor drain while trenching
Grind high spots and self-level the rest of the room before tile
